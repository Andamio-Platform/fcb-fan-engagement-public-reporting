# Milestone 2 Report

**Project:** FC Barcelona — Fan engagement infrastructure Cardano
**Milestone:** [M2 — Platform Setup](https://milestones.projectcatalyst.io/projects/1300101/milestones/2)

---

**A. Output:** Onboarding of the Barça Vision team — four complementary training sessions covering Andamio features and workflows. (The milestone outputs specify three sessions; four were delivered to provide broader coverage, each addressing a different aspect of the platform rather than repeating material.)

**Acceptance criteria:** 80% of the Barça Vision team completes onboarding within two weeks, achieved through training sessions covering all key features of Andamio. Evidence is provided as AI-generated meeting summaries and screenshots per session. Full session recordings are not shared publicly because they contain proprietary information; they can be made available to the Catalyst Team on request.

**Evidence:** https://github.com/Andamio-Platform/fcb-fan-engagement-public-reporting/tree/main/evidence/A-onboarding-training — four session screenshots and four Barça Vision-reviewed AI notes (filenames marked `_revised` reflect BV edits to the AI-generated text). Approval of these notes and screenshots is recorded in the M2 closing email from Barça Vision: https://github.com/Andamio-Platform/fcb-fan-engagement-public-reporting/blob/main/evidence/M2-Sign-off-email-BV-2026-05-14.pdf
